Question 7 (suggested time: 20 minutes)

Complete the bass line for the melody below, following eighteenth-century voice-leading procedures. Under the bass line, write the Roman and Arabic numerals that show the harmonies and inversions implied by the soprano and bass. Start where the arrow points.

  1. Write an appropriate cadence at the end of each phrase (fermata), including a perfect authentic cadence at the end.
  2. Give the bass melodic interest; nonharmonic tones may only be unaccented passing and neighbor tones.
  3. Vary the motion of the bass against the soprano.
  4. Use mostly quarter notes (half notes to eighth notes where appropriate). Do not write alto and tenor.
